### F-033 — Every metric artifact is graded Level C whatever produced it; eight numbers typed at a keyboard sign as historical simulation with a Sharpe of 17.6 — **High**

**Claim.** `metric_artifact` stamps `EvidenceLevel.HISTORICAL` unconditionally.
Nothing on the path can distinguish a series fetched from a source from numbers
invented at a call site, so a hypothesis is graded as historical simulation —
one rung up, in the flattering direction.

**Evidence.**
- `framework/metrics/reporting.py:213` — `level=EvidenceLevel.HISTORICAL`,
  hardcoded inside `EvidenceRecord.create` at `:210-217`, for every caller.
- `framework/artifacts/evidence.py:20-29` — `EvidenceRecord.create` takes
  `source` as a free string and `level` as whatever it is handed. It checks the
  timestamp's timezone and the confidence's bounds (`:36-48`) and neither of
  those two.
- `framework/metrics/reporting.py:124-133` — `Disclosure.data_source` is any
  non-blank string.
- Constructed and run against `main` at `84eed17`:

  ```
  validates: True
  evidence_level: EvidenceLevel.HISTORICAL
  signed: 4fc78dd6e53ab860
  value: 17.64352905875292
  data_source: I made these up
  known_limitations: none
  sample_period: 1990-01-01 -> 2026-01-01
  ```

  Eight numbers chosen by hand, `sharpe_artifact(...)`, `ArtifactValidator()`
  accepts it. A signed Level C artifact carrying a Sharpe of 17.6 over a
  declared 36-year window, disclosing "none" as its known limitations and no
  survivorship caveat at all.

**Breach.** `constitution/Evidence_Standards.md:39` — "Evidence hierarchy may
never be reversed." `:25` defines Level C as "Historical simulation"; `:33`
defines Level D as "Hypothesis. Research only." Arithmetic over invented numbers
is the second, labelled as the first. Also `:45`.

**The 2026-08-21 pass looked at this and let it go**, at
`reports/review/2026-08-21-review.md:802-810`: "it hardcodes
`level=EvidenceLevel.HISTORICAL` — Level C, **correct for a computation over a
supplied series**… with one producer, correctly stamped, there is no path to
exercise it, so it is recorded here rather than raised as a finding." That is
the comfortable reading, and it is the one Phase 4 of this skill asks to be
attacked. A supplied series need not have been supplied by anything. "One
producer, correctly stamped" describes the producer's *intent*; the grade is
stamped on the caller's input, and the caller is unconstrained.

**Failure.** *Fact for the construction, prediction for the cost.* No strategy
and no capital exist, so nothing has been decided on such an artifact yet. The
cost arrives at Stage 3: the backtester's first job is to emit exactly these
artifacts, and `constitution/Capital_Authority.md:11-14` makes `Statistical
edge` one of six inputs determining capital. A promotion gate reading
`evidence_level` sees `C` and cannot see that the series was never fetched. The
grade is the one field on the artifact that a gate is entitled to trust without
reading the content, and it is the one field nothing verifies.

**Proposal.** Smallest change that resolves it, and it is a ruling rather than an
edit: grade from provenance rather than from the emitter. `metric_artifact`
takes the evidence level from its `Disclosure`, and `Disclosure` gains a
constructor split — `disclosure_from(source, series, …)`
(`framework/data/fetch_record.py:446`) yields `HISTORICAL`, and a hand-built
`Disclosure` yields `RESEARCH`. That is one enum value threaded through one
function and requires no new machinery, and it makes F-034's ROADMAP claim true
as a side effect. It needs the owner's ruling because it decides what Level C
means.

  raised all three of the *then*-known items as one finding under that report's
