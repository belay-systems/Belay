---

> **Reconciled to the Constitution 2026-07-31 by ADR-006.** `Operational Stability`
> became `Operational consistency` and `Risk` became `Risk-adjusted returns`, both
> named explicitly by ADR-006 rule 3, which makes the wording of
> `constitution/Promotion_Pipeline.md:75-81` canonical. `Regime Robustness` and
> `Liquidity` already matched. Casing was left as this document writes it; ADR-006
> ruled on which word, not on house style.
>
> **The Promotion Score above is suspended, not adopted** — ADR-006 rule 4. It has a
> 0-100 range, no weights, no mapping from the eight categories, and defers its
> threshold entirely to the Investment Committee, so nothing can compute it. A number
> that looks computed but is not is worse than no number. It becomes live when the
> Committee supplies weights and a threshold.
>
> **Two divergences this reconciliation did not settle, recorded rather than guessed:**
>
> `Execution Consistency` above versus the Constitution's `Execution quality`.
> ADR-006 read all five lists and enumerated exactly three naming collisions; this is
> not among them. It either judged the two to be different concepts — consistency of
> execution is a track record, quality of execution is a standard — or missed it.
> Renaming on the general principle would be extending a ruling rather than applying
> one.
>
> `Drawdown behavior` is absent from this list. ADR-006's own context calls that out:
> `Drawdown` appears in four of the five lists and is missing from this one, "the one
> document that defines a Promotion Score". Rule 5 rules omissions to be defects but
> enumerates only `workflows/Monthly/StrategyPromotion.md`'s three.
